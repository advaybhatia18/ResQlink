LIVE PLATFORM MODULES

End-to-end emergency workflow

  • Fast SOS Reporting: Idempotent submission with GPS accuracy checks and no-GPS fallback.
  • Service Recommendations: Category + severity rules for Police, Fire & Rescue, and Medical.
  • Emergency Contacts: Primary contact management, tel: calls, and consent-audited fallback alerts.
  • Admin Command Center: Leaflet cluster map, status lifecycle, notes, and audit logs.
  • Isolated Simulator: 14 synthetic failure & incident scenarios completely separated from live alerts.

HOW IT WORKS

Four steps from report to resolution

1

Authenticate

Sign in via Supabase Auth. Profiles and row-level ownership keep personal alerts and contacts private.

2

Classify & Select

Pick the incident category and severity (1–5). The C engine computes priority while rules recommend services.

3

Send with GPS

Attach device GPS with accuracy verification or submit safely without coordinates if GPS is blocked.

4

Track & Coordinate

Track status history in your Emergency History while verified admins manage incidents in the Command Center.
